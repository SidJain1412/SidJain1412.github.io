Ribbit Account Deletion

Last updated: September 17, 2026

How to request deletion

Send an email to sidjain1412@gmail.com with the subject “Ribbit account deletion request.” Include the email address associated with your Ribbit account so we can verify the request and identify the account to delete.

We will confirm the request and process it within a reasonable period. We may ask for additional information needed to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password.

What will be deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete your Ribbit account and associated information processed through Firebase Authentication, including the account email address. We will also delete any Ribbit account information that we control and can reasonably associate with that account.

Recordings and field-log information saved locally in the Ribbit app are controlled from the app on your phone. Delete saved recordings from the Field log, or clear the app's data or uninstall the app, to remove that local data.

What may be retained

Firebase Analytics data may be retained in an aggregated or de-identified form when it can no longer reasonably be linked to your account. We may retain information when required by law, needed to establish or defend legal claims, or necessary to prevent abuse and fraud; any retained information will be kept only for the period required for that purpose.

Anything you previously shared with another person or app is controlled by the recipient and may not be recoverable or deletable by Ribbit. Please contact the recipient directly to request its removal.
