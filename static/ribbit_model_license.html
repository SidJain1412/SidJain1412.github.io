BirdNET-derived model package

The Ribbit Android candidate uses BirdNET V2.4. Ribbit modified the deployment graph to expose tensor 545 and trained a separate supported-species plus UNSURE head. Other Ribbit models

A model trained from third-party recordings may have additional source-licence obligations. Ribbit will not publish or represent a Ribbit-only model as separately redistributable before its versioned data manifest has completed a licence audit. Using an official app release does not grant a separate redistribution or commercial-use licence for such an artifact.

Limitations

A model result is a cautious lead, not a confirmed identification. UNSURE means no confident match among the supported pilot species; it does not mean no frog or provide a general unknown-sound guarantee.
