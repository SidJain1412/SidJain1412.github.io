Ribbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Ribbit is a non-profit, non-commercial citizen-science app made by Siddharth Jain. This policy explains what the current Android pilot collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you create or use a Ribbit account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password. Ribbit uses this account to provide access to the app. Ribbit does not receive or store your password in readable form.

When you record a sound, the app may collect and store on your phone:

  • the audio recording;
  • the recording date, duration, file size, and audio-quality information;
  • the phone's available location, including latitude, longitude, and location accuracy; and
  • Ribbit's model result and any observation notes that you enter.

Microphone and location access are used only when you choose to record. The model analyzes recordings on your phone. Recordings and field-log entries are not automatically uploaded to Ribbit's servers.

Ribbit also uses Firebase Analytics. Analytics may collect app and device information and events such as sign-in, screen views, recording-analysis results, sharing, observation updates, and exports. These analytics help us understand pilot use and improve the app. Analytics data is processed by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy.

Sharing

Ribbit shares a recording, report, or field-log export only when you choose the Android share option. A shared report can include the recording, exact location coordinates, date, model result, and your notes. A field-log export can also include the email address associated with your Ribbit account.

After you share something, the receiving app and its provider control how it is handled. Review the recipient and the receiving app before sharing. Ribbit does not sell your personal information or recordings.

Storage and deletion

Saved recordings and field-log information remain in the app's private storage on your phone until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Temporary recordings that are not saved are removed by the app after processing.

You can delete saved recordings from the Field log. To request deletion of your Ribbit account and associated information processed through Firebase, follow the Ribbit account deletion instructions. Information already shared with another person or app may not be recoverable by Ribbit.

Security

We use Android private app storage for saved recordings and Firebase services for account authentication and analytics. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please avoid recording private conversations and check a file before sharing it.

Children

Ribbit is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Ribbit's data practices change. The updated version will show a new "Last updated" date.
